FTL Smart Dispatch Co-pilot

A co-pilot, not a black box. It checks the data, predicts acceptance, and ranks carriers — the dispatcher still makes the final call.

Wed / Thu dispatch ritualFriday fine-tuningDispatcher decides

1 · Data Guardrail — Rate Check

Stops a bad rate from entering OTM. Flags any new rate that deviates from the historical average by more than the threshold.

2 · Acceptance Prediction

History + price competitiveness + peak-day pressure. Result is clipped to 5%–99%.

3 · Carrier Ranking — Weighted Score

Cheapest is not automatically best. Cost, commitment gap and predicted acceptance are scored together.

Total Score = w₁ × Cost Score + w₂ × Commitment Score + w₃ × Acceptance Probability  ·  ×100

Weights sum: 100%
